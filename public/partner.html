What Is the Twinsure Partner Program?

The Twinsure Partner Program allows individuals, professionals, and network communities to refer clients directly to Twinsure for insurance services. Whenever a referred customer successfully purchases a policy, the partner becomes eligible for a commission based on the insurance category and applicable terms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the Twinsure Partner Program.

How do commissions work?

Commissions are calculated as a percentage of the premium paid for the successfully referred policy. These rates depend on the insurance category and terms.

Who can join the program?

Anyone with a trusted network, including financial advisors, freelancers, working professionals, students, influencers, or community leaders.

When are commissions paid?

Once the referred customer's policy is successfully verified and the waiting period ends, earnings are credited and can be withdrawn directly to your linked bank account.

Is there any joining fee?

Absolutely not. The Twinsure Partner Program is 100% free to join. We believe in growing together without barrier entries.
